Read the sound, find the shape.

You get a pinyin and a meaning — never the character. Two to four look-alikes appear; exactly one of them is the one you were asked for. The tiles multiply and the clock shortens as the run goes on. Three misses and it is over.

or press Enter

Sets come from two places: a hand-curated list of the classic traps (己/已/巳, 请/清/情/晴, 十/千/干/土/士) and a similarity pass over every character in HSK 1–3 that scores shared components, shared radicals, stroke count and shared sound. A few distractors sit outside HSK 1–3 — they are only ever wrong answers, never the one you are asked for.
